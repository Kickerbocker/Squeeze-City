# Known unknowns

Questions we can't answer yet, and how each could be answered. Review at the end of every milestone. Anything open for two milestones gets a test or is marked "won't know".

| # | Opened | Question | Why it matters | How to find out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | 2026-10-09 | Are the audit thresholds right (85% lazy play, 30-day payback, 60% quiet days)? | They are our guesses. If they are wrong the audit passes boring builds or fails fun ones. | Compare audit results with the player's verdict over the next three playtests. |
| U2 | 2026-10-09 | How much math should be shown by default, and how much on request? | Benjamin answered the first half on 2026-10-09: show the math, or at least a before-and-after comparison for upgrades. What remains is how much detail fits a phone screen without feeling like a spreadsheet. | Research how other games show purchase effects; then test one upgrade card each way. |
| U3 | 2026-10-09 | Does a 1-minute day leave room for mid-day decisions without feeling rushed? | The player wants calm and cozy, a 1-minute day, and a reason not to skip. These pull against each other. | Prototype the day with two decision moments and playtest at 1 minute and 2 minutes. |
| U4 | 2026-10-09 | What keeps an endless sandbox worth returning to? | The player wants no final win but does want incentives. | Tear down how RollerCoaster Tycoon scenarios, Kairosoft and Restaurant City handle long-term goals. |
| U5 | 2026-10-09 | Is choosing a spot within a location fun, or one more setting to get right once? | The player asked for stand placement by foot traffic. A fixed best spot would be solved on day one. | Research how Lemonade Tycoon and other games vary traffic over time; add a never-moves bot. |
| U6 | 2026-10-09 | What should staff be for? | At v1 wages no role pays. At M9 wages a Server and a Promoter pay only at busy locations, and the Mixer pays nowhere because pitcher prep is a small share of serving time. Cheaper staff may just become a mandatory purchase. | Design pass on staff as jobs the player can see (M14), then the audit. |
| U7 | 2026-10-09 | Which of our problems would an experienced designer spot that we haven't? | Neither of us has built a game before. | One teardown per milestone; log every playtest surprise here. |
| U8 | 2026-10-09 | Can the sim replay a day without one purchase and give an honest difference? | **Mostly answered 2026-10-09: yes.** Each passer-by draws the same four random numbers whether or not they stop, so the same people arrive in both runs. A one-day test confirmed it for Neon sign, Juicer, Server, Flyers and Fridge. It fails for the Radio campaign, which adds foot traffic. An earlier version of this entry said an engine change was needed. That was wrong. | What remains: exact figures for Radio and TV. M10 shows them as a 7-day average. A fix is in `LATER.md`. |
| U9 | 2026-10-09 | How long should an upgrade take to pay for itself? | Two research passes gave different ranges (2 to 12 days, and 5 to 30). The audit uses 30. | Tune toward the overlap, then ask after a playtest which purchases felt worth it. |
| U10 | 2026-10-09 | Can storage upgrades (Cooler, Fridge) ever matter? | Ingredients are about 15% of the price of a cup, ice costs a cent a cube, and a sensible player never wastes lemons. No price makes these worth buying. M9 takes them off sale. | Decide whether waste should be a real risk (dearer ingredients, shorter shelf life, bulk deals worth chasing), then redesign or drop them for good. |
| U11 | 2026-10-09 | Is buying stock a decision or a chore? | Over-buying is punished (the Hoarder wastes 33% of revenue). Nobody has measured under-buying. | M9 adds a Skimper bot and reports the gap. Under 10% means it is a chore and should be automated or redesigned. |
| U12 | 2026-10-09 | Do the Newspaper, Radio and TV campaigns pay with several stands open? | They lose money with one stand, which is how the audit tests them. They are meant for several. | Audit them with two to four stands at M13. |
| U13 | 2026-10-10 | What music sounds "relaxing but upbeat and fun" to Benjamin? | The generated music read as ominous. Nobody had a reference. | He names one or two songs or soundtracks. Nothing is composed before that. |
| U14 | 2026-10-10 | Is a player who ignores the forecast punished too hard? | In the paper model, never changing batch or price earns 60% of careful play at the stand. That may be too harsh for a calm game. A handed-off stand earns 93%. | Playtest 3. If the mornings feel like pressure, narrow the weather's swing or raise what leftovers are worth. |
| U15 | 2026-10-10 | Does owning two things feel like growth or like twice the chores? | The hand-off rule is the riskiest idea in v2. Big Ambitions' players called older businesses "homework". | P2 and Playtest 4: "When the cart arrived, what did you stop thinking about?" |
| U16 | 2026-10-10 | What should going broke lead to? | Proposed: step down one stage and keep skills and recipes. | Benjamin's call (GDD-v2 section 14). |
| U17 | 2026-10-10 | Can free 3D kits rendered to 2D, mixed with AI-generated icons, look like one game? | The recommended art route has not been tried. | P3: one finished scene. |
| U18 | 2026-10-10 | Which real-world numbers hold up? | Vending, cart and food-truck figures came from blogs that disagree, and several stages were not sourced at all. | Check before any figure is shown to the player as realistic. The game's own dollars do not depend on them. |
| U19 | 2026-10-10 | Do rival vendors belong in the city? | They would give trends and prices a visible cause, and could add pressure a cozy game does not want. | Ask after Playtest 3. |
