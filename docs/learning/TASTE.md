# What Benjamin finds fun

His words, kept as he said them. This file outranks every rule and number elsewhere: the game has to be fun to him. Add to it after every playtest. Do not add guesses about what he would like.

## The game he wants

- A Lemonade Tycoon 2-style remake for nostalgia, not for sale. It should capture the idea, not match the original.
- "Calm and cozy but stimulates the brain."
- A day should take about 1 minute.
- Bad days are a "setback but bankruptcy is possible if finances are bad enough".
- "Endless sandbox but needs to have incentives."
- Visually appealing, in the direction of Lemonade Tycoon 2. Graphics, music and sound that feel like a full game.
- Skill trees and point allocation.
- More realistic about how an actual business runs.
- Choosing where the stand goes within an area, to work out what has more foot traffic.
- The day should be "really satisfying and fulfilling" to watch, with a mechanic that gives a reason not to skip. Skipping stays available.

## Games he likes

Lemonade Tycoon 2, RollerCoaster Tycoon, Game Dev Tycoon, Thrillville, Cities: Skylines, Kairosoft games, Restaurant City, Kingdom Rush.

## What he has disliked

- 2026-10-09: "I was bored when things got monotonous on the recipe."
- 2026-10-09: "There's little incentive to buy any upgrades or staff."
- 2026-10-09: "You have to hoard money a lot to move onto the next stage which required just skipping a lot of days."
- 2026-10-09: Upgrades, hiring and marketing "gave some description on the benefits", but "I really couldn't tell how much it was truly helping after I purchased them".
- 2026-10-09: "I didn't understand the purpose of buying multiple parts of the map or what makes it truly better."

- 2026-10-10: "The math didn't make sense much… Is it per customer or something?"
- 2026-10-10: "The music felt more ominous than relaxing."
- 2026-10-10: "I feel like the game didn't really change much day by day. Not much thinking or problem solving, and got mundane really quick."

## What he wants next (2026-10-10)

- "Start off as someone who want to hustle and start off small. Do small gigs, gain skills, build and earn money, buy other things that make money like vending machines or other side hustles all in the food space as a individual entrepreneur who only wants to work for him/herself."
- "Eventually a lemonade stand, then food stand, then food truck, restaurant, etc. with more curation of food recipes, finding trends or new taste palettes and or add new things to sell."
- "Maybe add some more game elements from the other referenced games such as restaurant city."
- Music: "a relaxing but upbeat and fun melody."
- Visuals: "way more appealing, similar to lemonade tycoon 2."
- "It's time to pick it up substantially. Much more research and development."

## How much the game should explain

- 2026-10-09: On upgrades: "Perhaps show the math, or at least show a comparison of before the upgrade and after."
- 2026-10-10: The M10 version of "Show the math" (multipliers like "×1.15 → ×1.35" and seven sample-day dollar figures) did not make sense to him. Explanations have to be in plain cause and effect, in his own numbers.
