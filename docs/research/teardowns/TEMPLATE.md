# Teardown: <game>

Question this teardown answers: <the design decision it informs>

Keep each field to a few sentences. Tag claims `[E]`, `[X]` or `[P]` as in `docs/research/PRINCIPLES.md`.

1. **Fantasy and feeling** - what the game makes the player feel
2. **Loops** - what the player does every few seconds, every few minutes, every few hours, and how each feeds the next
3. **Decisions** - each repeated decision: how often, how many real options, what the player knows beforehand, what feedback follows
4. **Economy** - where resources come from, where they go, what compounds
5. **Progression** - how often something new unlocks, and whether it changes what the player does or only the numbers
6. **Legibility** - how the game shows why a result happened
7. **Solved state** - what guides and forums say the best strategy is. Is it fun to execute?
8. **Where it fails** - complaints that repeat across reviews: boredom, grind, confusion, nothing left to do
9. **Take and avoid** - three mechanics worth adapting, one to avoid, each with the feeling it serves
10. **What this means for us** - which of our systems it affects, and what we would change
